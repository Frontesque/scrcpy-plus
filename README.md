### What's new in 3.0.0

- 🎛️ **Redesigned launch menu**
- 🖥️ **Display settings** • _Custom window name, crop, display ID, virtual display, rotation lock, screen recording and no-video mode_
- ⌨️ **Hide the on-screen keyboard** • _Type with a physical keyboard via `--keyboard=uhid`_
- ✍️ **Custom flags** • _Pass any other SCRCPY flag from the Advanced drawer_
- 💾 **Remember settings** • _Optionally restore your launch options on the next start_
- 📶 **Better wireless** • _Disconnect from the app and remember the last IP address_
- 🧰 **Bring your own binaries (Windows)** • _Pick your own `scrcpy.exe` and `adb.exe` in Settings_
- 🔒 **Unauthorized detection** • _Devices waiting for USB debugging approval are labelled_
- 🚀 **Upgraded to Nuxt 4 and Tauri 2**

### Features

- 🚩 **Supports Most SCRCPY Options**
- 📱 **Device Information** • _Get Model Information About the Active Device_
- 📶 **Wireless Connectivity** • _Connect to a Device on your WiFi Network_
- 🗣️ **Multi-Language Support** • _Use SCRCPY+ in your native language (English, Italiano, 日本語)_

### Prerequisites

- LINUX & Mac: Both SCRCPY & ADB need to be installed to your computer per [your distro's instructions](https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy#linux).


Some options need a recent SCRCPY: `--keyboard=uhid` needs 2.4+, virtual display needs 3.0+ and Android 10+, and audio features need 2.0+.

Want to build it yourself? See [BUILDING.md](BUILDING.md).
